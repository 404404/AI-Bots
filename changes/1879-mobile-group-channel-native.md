## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a group Channel now opens as a native chat like a DM, instead of the embedded full client: replies stream in, each Bot's name sits above its messages, `@` mentions a member Bot or All Bots, cards can be answered in place, a working Bot can be stopped, and notifications for the Channel open it directly ([#1879](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1879)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里的群聊频道现在像私信一样以原生聊天页打开，不再进入内嵌的完整客户端：回复会实时流式显示，每个 Bot 的名字显示在它的消息上方，输入 `@` 可以提及成员 Bot 或所有 Bot，审批和提问卡片可以直接处理，可以停止正在工作的 Bot，频道的通知也会直接打开这个聊天（[#1879](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1879)）。
