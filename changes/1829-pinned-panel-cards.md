## en

- On desktop, the Bot mode sidebar now shows a row of small cards, one per panel, like a browser's pinned tabs. A card is highlighted while its panel is open, and clicking it opens or closes that panel next to the Channel. Activity Center is now one of these panels, so it can stay open beside a conversation; its card replaces the old Activity Center row and keeps the unread count ([#1829](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1829)).

## zh

- 桌面 Bot 模式侧栏现在有一排小卡片，每个面板一张，就像浏览器的固定标签。面板打开时卡片会高亮，点一下就在 Channel 旁打开或关闭这个面板。活动中心也成了其中一个面板，可以一直开在对话旁边；它的卡片取代原来的活动中心一行，并保留未读数（[#1829](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1829)）。
