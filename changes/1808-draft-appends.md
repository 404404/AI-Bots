## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now receives a Bot's streaming reply as appended text instead of the whole reply on every update, cutting the data a long reply sends to the phone by about ten times. Older mobile apps and the Web Client keep receiving whole drafts ([#1808](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1808)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 接收 Bot 的流式回复时，现在只收新增的文字，不再每次更新都收整段回复；一条长回复发到手机的数据量约减少到十分之一。旧版手机 App 和 Web Client 仍然收到完整草稿（[#1808](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1808)）。
