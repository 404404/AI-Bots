## en

- Mobile Activity now separates actions, personal messages and other unread conversations into collapsible sections on the selected Host, with a floating header for search and combined filtering/sorting, native iOS glass controls and platform-specific option sheets that apply immediately, a single search clear control and no empty sections in search results, and readable dark-mode status bars, paged results, and handled history available through its filter ([#1954](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1954)).

## zh

- 手机 Activity 现在将当前 Host 的待处理事项、与你有关的消息和其他未读会话分为可折叠的分组，通过悬浮顶部栏搜索及统一筛选/排序，采用 iOS 原生玻璃控件和各平台适配且即时生效的选项面板，搜索只保留清空按钮并隐藏无匹配的分组，修正深色状态栏对比度，支持分页，已处理历史通过筛选查看（[#1954](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1954)）。
