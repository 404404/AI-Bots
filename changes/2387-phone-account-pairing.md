## en

- **Pair a phone with your DeepSeekBot account**: the phone's pairing screen can sign in to the same DeepSeekBot account as your computer, list the computers linked to it and pair with one tap, without a QR code or Tailscale. The offer travels through `deepseekbot.dev` and only the phone that asked for it can redeem it ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).

## zh

- **用 DeepSeekBot 账号配对手机**：手机的配对页可以登录和电脑相同的 DeepSeekBot 账号，列出关联的电脑，点一下就能配对，不用扫码，也不用 Tailscale。配对码经 `deepseekbot.dev` 传递，只有发起请求的那台手机能兑换（[#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)）。
