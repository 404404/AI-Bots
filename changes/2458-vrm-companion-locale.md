## en

- The VRM Companion panel now follows the app language: its title (VRM 伴侣), header state, menus, loading text, errors and the no-GPU notice appear in Chinese when the app is in Chinese, and switch without reopening the panel. Without graphics acceleration the panel now shows a still of the character and a clear **Show 3D anyway** button ([#2458](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2458)).

## zh

- VRM Companion 面板现在跟随应用语言：面板标题（VRM 伴侣）、标题栏状态、菜单、加载提示、错误提示和没有显卡时的说明，在中文界面下都会显示为中文，切换语言后无需重新打开面板。没有显卡加速时，面板会显示一张角色静态图，并提供明显的「仍然开启 3D」按钮（[#2458](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2458)）。
