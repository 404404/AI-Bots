## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, the **Computer** button of a Bot that uses this computer now opens a native screen showing the desktop live. **Pause Bot and take control** stops the Bot from acting or taking screenshots and lets you click, scroll and type from the phone, with a password keyboard for logins. The new `deepseekbot computer-frame`, `computer-takeover` and `computer-input` commands do the same from a terminal ([#1832](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1832)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里，使用本机 Computer 的 Bot 的**Computer**按钮现在会打开原生界面，实时显示桌面。点**暂停 Bot 并接管**后，Bot 不能操作也不能截图，你可以在手机上点击、滚动和输入，登录时可用密码键盘。新的 `deepseekbot computer-frame`、`computer-takeover` 和 `computer-input` 命令可以在终端做同样的事（[#1832](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1832)）。
