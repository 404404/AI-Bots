## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a DM's header now blends into the chat with no bar of its own. Back sits on the left, and a round chip in the middle shows the Bot's avatar, its name and a dot for its state: idle, working, waiting for you or blocked. The chat runs under the header and down to the bottom of the screen, with the header and the composer floating over it like Telegram: messages show faintly through the header chip and pass behind the composer, fading softly at both edges, and the Model and Workspace chips moved from under the composer into a Bot settings group on the Bot profile ([#1875](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1875)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊页的顶栏与聊天背景融为一体，不再有单独的栏。左侧是返回按钮，中间是一个圆角胶囊，依次显示 Bot 的头像、名字和表示状态的小圆点：空闲、工作中、等你处理或受阻。聊天内容从顶栏下方一直延伸到屏幕底部，顶栏和输入框像 Telegram 一样浮在消息上方，消息能隐约透过顶部胶囊看到，也会从输入框后面穿过，并在上下两端柔和地渐隐；模型和工作区不再显示在输入框下方，改到 Bot 资料页的“Bot 设置”分组里（[#1875](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1875)）。
