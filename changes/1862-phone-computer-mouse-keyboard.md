## en

- The phone's Computer screen now drives the desktop like a remote-desktop app. Drag to move a large arrow pointer, then use the left and right buttons or roll the scroll wheel under it. Keyboard mode opens the phone's own keyboard with a bar of sticky ⌘ ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ keys, Return, Tab and Escape. Control switches between trackpad and direct tap. Typing from the phone now reaches the Mac's frontmost app (it was refused before), letters no longer double, and inputs arrive in the order you send them ([#1862](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1862)).

## zh

- 手机上的 Computer 页面现在可以像远程桌面 App 一样操作电脑：拖动来移动大号箭头指针，再用左键、右键，或在指针处拖动滚轮。键盘模式会拉起手机自带键盘，上方有可锁定的 ⌘ ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ 和回车、Tab、Esc 键。“控制”里可以在触控板和直接点按之间切换。手机打的字现在能送到 Mac 最前面的 App（之前会被拒绝），字母不会再重复，输入也会按发送顺序到达（[#1862](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1862)）。
