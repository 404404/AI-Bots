## en

- Names in the chat list and in **Send to** no longer get cut short, as in "QA Loun…", after switching between light and dark on Android. With larger text, chat-list and Activity names and previews wrap to a second line instead of ending in "…" early, the time beside a name always shows in full, and a long computer name in the tabs above the chat list can use up to about three-fifths of the screen before it is shortened ([#2443](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2443)).

## zh

- Android 上切换浅色/深色主题后，会话列表和「发送到」里的名称不会再被截成 “QA Loun…” 这样。调大字体后，会话列表和动态里的名称与预览会换到第二行，不再过早显示 “…”，名称旁的时间始终完整显示；会话列表上方的设备标签在名称较长时，最多可占约五分之三的屏幕宽度，超过才省略（[#2443](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2443)）。
