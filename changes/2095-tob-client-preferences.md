## en

- Tob can discover connected Clients and change the selected browser's notification preference through reviewed CLI commands, reporting OS permission separately. Appearance, Human name and IM defaults reuse their existing owners and disclose their shared Host/Profile scope ([#2095](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2095), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-client-preferences)).

## zh

- Tob 可发现已连接的 Client，并通过经审核的 CLI 命令修改选中浏览器的通知偏好，单独报告系统权限。外观、Human 默认名称及 IM 默认设置沿用现有 owner，并明确说明共享的 Host／Profile 作用域（[#2095](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2095)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-client-preferences)）。
