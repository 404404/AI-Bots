## en

- Panel scrollbars appear while their content scrolls, then fade after a short pause without changing content width. Each scroll area tracks its own activity; reduced motion hides the thumb without animation ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

## zh

- 面板滚动条仅在内容滚动时显示，停顿后自动淡出，内容宽度保持不变。各滚动区域独立计时，减少动效模式直接隐藏滑块（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。
