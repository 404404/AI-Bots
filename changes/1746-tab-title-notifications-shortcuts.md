## en

- The browser tab title now shows the Human Inbox unread count, with a ● mark while something waits for your decision, for example `● (3) DeepSeek Harness` ([#1746](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1746)).
- Desktop notifications: turn on **Desktop notifications** in Bot settings › General, and while the page is in the background this browser shows a system notification when a Bot needs your approval or an answer, mentions or replies to you, or finishes replying in a DM. Clicking it opens the message ([#1747](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1747)).
- Press `?` or Ctrl+/ (⌘+/ on Mac) in Bot mode to see every keyboard shortcut; the list is also under Bot settings › General ([#1748](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1748)).

## zh

- 浏览器标签页标题会显示 Human Inbox 的未读数，有事等你决定时前面加 ●，例如 `● (3) DeepSeek Harness`（[#1746](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1746)）。
- 桌面通知：在 Bot 设置 › 通用里打开「桌面通知」后，页面在后台时，Bot 需要你审批或回答、@你、回复你，或在私聊里回复完成，这个浏览器会弹出系统通知，点击即跳到对应消息（[#1747](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1747)）。
- 在 Bot mode 按 `?` 或 Ctrl+/（Mac 上 ⌘+/）查看全部键盘快捷键，也可以在 Bot 设置 › 通用里打开（[#1748](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1748)）。
