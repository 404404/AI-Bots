## en

- Tob can end a Group through reviewed CLI confirmation while preserving history, and upload a selected local attachment without sending it. Both paths recheck Human authority and retain existing owner validation ([#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-deletion-and-attachment-upload)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过经审核、显式确认的 CLI 结束群组并保留历史，也可上传指定本地附件而不发送消息。两条路径均复查 Human 权限并保留现有服务校验（[#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-deletion-and-attachment-upload)）。
