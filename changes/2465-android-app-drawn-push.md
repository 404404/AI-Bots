## en

- Android phone notifications from a Bot now show the Bot's avatar, the Channel picture and approval buttons when the app is in the background. The Host sends a new app build data-only pushes so the app draws them; older builds keep the plain system notification. Needs a new app build ([#2465](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2465)).
