## en

- Navigation shortcuts can be changed: open the shortcut list (`?` or Ctrl/⌘+/), choose **Change** next to Bot mode, quick switcher, previous/next (unread) conversation or the list itself, and press the new keys. A shortcut needs Ctrl/⌘ or Alt, cannot reuse one that is taken or reserved, and **Reset** brings back the default. On macOS the list shows ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ([#1775](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1775)).

## zh

- 导航快捷键可以修改：打开快捷键列表（`?` 或 Ctrl/⌘+/），在 Bot 模式、快速切换、上/下一个（未读）会话或快捷键列表旁点「修改」，再按下新的组合键。组合需要包含 Ctrl/⌘ 或 Alt，不能和已占用或保留的组合重复，点「恢复默认」可还原。macOS 上显示 ⌘ ⌥ ⇧（[#1775](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1775)）。
