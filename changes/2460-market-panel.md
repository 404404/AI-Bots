## en

- **Bot Marketplace panel** in the workspace controls: a flat grid of Marketplace Bots that follows the panel width, with search that runs when you press Enter, sorting by newest, recently updated or most stars, **Import** on every card (through the same install confirmation), and **Upload Bot**, which opens a Bot's DM with the Share a Bot publishing steps filled in or lists a public repository you already have. The Marketplace modal now also searches on Enter only, and `deepseekbot marketplace-list --sort created` lists the newest repositories first ([#2460](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2460)).

## zh

- **Bot 市场面板**加入工作区控制栏：用随面板宽度排布的扁平卡片浏览市场 Bot，搜索在按回车时才执行，可按最新创建、最近更新或最多 Star 排序；每张卡片都能**导入**（沿用安装确认），**上传 Bot** 会打开所选 Bot 的私聊并填好“分享 Bot”的发布步骤，或直接收录已有的公开仓库。Bot 市场弹窗的搜索也改为按回车才执行；`deepseekbot marketplace-list --sort created` 按仓库创建时间从新到旧列出（[#2460](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2460)）。
