## en

- A tool call whose input is longer than 16 KB now shows an approval card instead of being refused, and a typed answer on a question card is no longer limited to 2,000 characters. Bot-mode cards now accept what DSH itself accepts ([#1648](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1648)).

## zh

- 参数超过 16KB 的工具调用现在会出审批卡，不再被直接拒绝；提问卡里手写的回答也不再限制在 2000 字以内。Bot 模式的卡片现在和 DSH 本身接受的内容一致（[#1648](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1648)）。
