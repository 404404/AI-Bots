## en

- Bot Settings now searches settings across pages and jumps to the matching control. Appearance groups theme, motion, Bot icon, language and workspace layout; Messaging groups your default name and send-key preference, Notifications has its own page, and Keyboard shortcuts can be filtered and edited inline. Shift+Enter sending is available alongside existing options. The tutorial introduces the Panel workspace, and Chinese Browser/Computer settings use localized names ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

## zh

- Bot 设置支持跨页面搜索并定位具体设置。界面设置集中管理主题、动效、Bot 图标、语言和工作区布局；消息页管理默认名字和发送按键；通知独立成页，键盘快捷键可直接搜索和编辑。发送方式新增 Shift+Enter，并保留已有选项。新手教程介绍面板工作区，Browser/Computer 设置已本地化为浏览器控制和电脑控制（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。
