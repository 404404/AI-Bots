## en

- Form and confirmation dialogs now share one layout. Each field shows its label above and its hint or error below, every form ends with Cancel and one main action, Enter submits, and destructive confirmations use a red button. This covers creating and renaming Bots, channels and sections, schedules, IM app, identity and connector setup, profile editing, nicknames, member wake policy, Bot deletion, zip import and export, history purge and diagnostics. Dialogs that only report a result show a single button. Group management in Channel settings is now two rows, and the group name saves when you press Enter or leave the field ([#1600](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1600)).

## zh

- 表单和确认类对话框统一成一种布局：标签在上，提示或错误在下；每个表单都以「取消」加一个主操作结尾，按回车即可提交；危险操作的确认按钮是红色的。覆盖创建和重命名 Bot、频道与分组，定时任务，IM 应用、身份与连接器设置，资料编辑，昵称，成员唤醒策略，删除 Bot，zip 导入导出，清除历史和诊断包。只告知结果的对话框只保留一个按钮。频道设置里的「群管理」改为两行，群名按回车或离开输入框就保存（[#1600](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1600)）。
