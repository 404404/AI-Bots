## en

- List and export a Bot's portable files through the CLI, including reviewed Tob requests with exact file/history scope, content-change refusal and a new private output file. Exported Tob personality imports as an ordinary Bot without management authority ([#2089](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2089), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#bot-zip-file-listing-and-export)).

## zh
