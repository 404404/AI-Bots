## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now has native Home, Activity and Me tabs. Home lists PersonaBot DMs and Channels together by latest activity, marks the ones that need you, and has Needs you and Working cards that filter the list; Activity is the former Inbox; Me shows the current computer, the full client, your computers, notifications and unpairing. The computer switcher moves to a tile at the top left of Home ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 改用原生的首页、动态、我三个 Tab。首页把 PersonaBot 私信和频道按最近活动合在一个列表里，标出等你处理的对话，并用“等你处理”“工作中”两张卡片筛选列表；动态即原来的收件箱；“我”里是当前电脑、完整客户端、电脑列表、通知和取消配对；切换电脑改为首页左上角的图标（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
