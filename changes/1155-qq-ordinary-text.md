## en

- QQ's checked consumer can forward ordinary group text for fresh receiver qualification and explicit per-conversation collection. Existing Observation and count/time harvest keep collection separate from intentional own-app posting; old events from blocked intake intervals do not qualify a newly allowed conversation. The product includes the qualified Provider as independently versioned `4.32.0-botharness.21`; real application and Client acceptance remain separately tracked ([#1155](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1155)).
