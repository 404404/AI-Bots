## en

- On iPhone, a streaming reply now redraws only its last paragraph as text arrives instead of re-rendering the whole reply each time, so long replies stay smoother while they stream and while you scroll ([#1845](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1845)).

## zh

- iPhone 上，流式回复每来一段字只重绘最后一段，不再整条回复重新渲染，长回复在输出和边输出边滑动时更流畅（[#1845](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1845)）。
