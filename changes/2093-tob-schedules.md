## en

- Tob can manage a selected Bot’s scheduled work through reviewed CLI commands, showing its timezone, next occurrence and actual execution history. Startup catch-up waits for the native credentials Service and safely resumes unobserved scheduled work without prior side effects. Creation retries recover the original task; stale state, locks and destructive confirmation remain enforced ([#2093](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2093), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-scheduled-work)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过经审核的 CLI 命令管理选定 Bot 的定时工作，展示时区、下次运行与实际执行历史；启动补跑等待原生凭据服务就绪，并安全恢复未观察且没有既有副作用的定时工作；创建重试核对原任务，并继续检查过期状态、锁定及删除确认（[#2093](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2093)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-scheduled-work)）。
