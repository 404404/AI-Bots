## en

- A PersonaBot's Browser and Computer entries now work inside the DeepSeekBot mobile app's full client: you can watch the live view, take over and hand back, and use the start, stop and settings controls from your phone. Revoking the phone ends these viewers at once too ([ADR-0199](../docs/adr/0199-paired-devices-reach-the-host-through-a-same-listener-device-gateway.md), [#1687](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1687)).

## zh

- PersonaBot 的「浏览器」和「电脑」入口现在可以在 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的完整网页端里使用：在手机上就能看实时画面、接管和交还，并使用启动、停止和设置等控制。撤销这台手机后，这些画面也会立即断开（[ADR-0199](../docs/adr/0199-paired-devices-reach-the-host-through-a-same-listener-device-gateway.md)，[#1687](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1687)）。
