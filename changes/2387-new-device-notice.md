## en

- **New-device notice**: when a device pairs with your computer, your other paired phones get a notification with a **Remove it** button that revokes the new device in one tap, and Settings → Devices on the computer marks devices paired in the last day ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).

## zh

- **新设备提醒**：有设备和电脑配对后，其他已配对的手机会收到通知，点**移除**即可撤销这台新设备；电脑上的设置 → 设备也会标出最近一天内配对的设备（[#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)）。
