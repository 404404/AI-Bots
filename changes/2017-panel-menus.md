## en

- The Control Rail context menu lets you choose which Panel switches appear; hiding a switch also closes its Panel and remembers the choice. Panel headers now offer Close in their context menu. Rail tooltips show the current customizable shortcut as keycaps outside the rail's clipping boundary ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

- Pointer menus now share viewport-aware positioning across Channels, messages, members, files and memory history, preventing down/right drift under interface zoom ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

## zh

- 面板控制列右键菜单支持选择显示哪些面板开关；隐藏开关时会同时关闭对应面板，并记住选择。面板标题右键菜单支持关闭面板。控制列悬停提示以按键样式显示当前自定义快捷键，并避免被列边界裁切（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。

- 频道、消息、成员、文件和记忆历史的右键菜单统一使用视口定位，修复界面缩放时向右下方偏移的问题（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。
