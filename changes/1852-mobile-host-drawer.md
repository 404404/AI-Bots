## en

- In the mobile app, switching computers now happens in a left drawer: tap the computer name at the top of Home, or swipe right from the left edge of Home or Activity. Each computer shows whether it is online, with separate badges for items that need you and unread conversations, and a ⋯ menu to unpair it; the footer keeps "Pair a new computer" and Notifications ([#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)).

## zh

- 手机应用改为在左侧抽屉里切换电脑：点首页顶部的电脑名称，或在首页、动态页从左边缘向右滑即可打开。每台电脑会显示是否在线，并分别用两个角标显示等你处理和未读会话的数量，⋯ 菜单可取消配对；底部固定“配对新电脑”和“通知”（[#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)）。
