## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, the mic in a DM's composer now turns speech into text on the phone. Hold the mic to talk and release to stop, slide left to cancel, or slide up (or tap) to keep listening hands-free. The words fill the message field, so you can edit them before sending. If the microphone or speech recognition is not allowed, the chat says so ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊输入框的麦克风现在可以在手机上把语音转成文字。按住麦克风说话、松开结束，左滑取消，上滑（或点按）锁定后无需按住。文字会填进输入框，发送前可以修改。如果没有允许麦克风或语音识别，聊天里会给出提示（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
