## en

- Tob can create groups of existing Bots and invite another Bot through reviewed CLI operations. Stable operation keys recover the original group or invitation after a lost response; invitations retain the configured acceptance policy and Tob stays outside managed groups ([#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-creation-and-invitations)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过经审核的 CLI 操作创建现有 Bot 的群聊，并邀请其他 Bot。响应丢失后，稳定操作标识可核对原群或邀请；邀请沿用已配置的接受策略，Tob 自身不加入受管理群聊（[#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-creation-and-invitations)）。
