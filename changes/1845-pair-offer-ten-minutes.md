## en

- The pairing QR code under Settings → Devices now stays valid for 10 minutes instead of 5, so there is time to install the DeepSeekBot app, allow local network access and scan ([#1845](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1845)).

## zh

- 「设置 → 设备」里的配对二维码有效期从 5 分钟延长到 10 分钟，安装 DeepSeekBot App、允许本地网络访问后再扫码也来得及（[#1845](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1845)）。
