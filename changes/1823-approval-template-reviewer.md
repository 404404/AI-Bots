## en

- A PersonaBot can now select an Approval Template, "Cautious" or "Get the work done", in its workspace settings or with `deepseekbot approval-template-set`. While one is selected, an Approval Reviewer answers the Bot's tool approvals by the template before any card is posted: it allows or rejects a call and leaves a folded record in the Channel, or sends the call on to you as the usual card when it is unsure or fails. Changing a Bot's template always needs your own approval ([#1823](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1823)).

## zh

- PersonaBot 现在可以在工作区设置里或用 `deepseekbot approval-template-set` 选择一个自动审批模板（“谨慎”或“把活干完”）。选中后，审批员会先按模板回答此 Bot 的工具审批，再决定是否发卡片：它批准或拒绝的调用会在 Channel 里留下一条折叠记录；它拿不准或出错时，仍按原来的卡片交给你。修改 Bot 的模板永远需要你本人批准（[#1823](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1823)）。
