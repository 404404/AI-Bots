## en

- Added `deepseekbot connect-sign-in` and `connect-sign-out`: a Host signs in to a WorkOS account with CLI Auth, links itself through the Connect relay and runs the tunnel the relay provisions, recovering it after reclamation on the same hostname ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).
