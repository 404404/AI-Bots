## en

- The chat’s right dock gains a Bot files panel with per-Bot attachment history, including older messages. Files open through the existing attachment viewer and can be referenced through the composer. Selections survive timeline navigation and draft recovery, share the ten-file upload limit, and can be removed without deleting the file ([#1960](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1960)).

## zh

- 聊天右侧 Dock 新增「Bot 文件」面板，按 Bot 展示附件历史，包括较早的消息。文件沿用既有附件查看器打开，并可在输入框中引用。所选文件在切换历史窗口和恢复草稿后保留，与上传文件共用十个文件的上限；取消引用不会删除文件（[#1960](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1960)）。
