## en

- The phone's chat composer now follows each platform's own compose bar. On iOS, **+** is its own round button beside a separate field capsule with the mic or Send inside, like iOS 26 Messages; on iOS 26 both float on Liquid Glass over the messages. On Android, **+** is a Material 3 icon button next to a filled rounded field, with ripple feedback. On iOS 26, messages under the composer now soften with the system's own scroll edge effect, matching the header ([#2355](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2355)).

## zh

- 手机端聊天输入框改为各平台原生的输入栏样式。iOS 上，**+** 是独立的圆形按钮，旁边是单独的输入胶囊，麦克风或发送按钮在胶囊内，与 iOS 26 信息 App 一致；在 iOS 26 上两者以液态玻璃悬浮在消息上方。Android 上，**+** 是 Material 3 图标按钮，旁边是填充圆角输入框，点按有水波纹反馈。在 iOS 26 上，输入框下方的消息改用系统自带的滚动边缘效果柔化，与顶栏一致（[#2355](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2355)）。
