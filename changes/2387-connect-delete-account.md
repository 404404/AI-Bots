## en

- **Delete your DeepSeekBot account from the phone**: the pairing sheet's account section has **Delete account**. After you confirm, the DeepSeekBot Connect relay unlinks every computer signed in to the account, deletes their tunnels and addresses, and deletes the account itself ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).

## zh

- **在手机上删除 DeepSeekBot 账号**：配对页的账号区域新增「删除账号」。确认后，DeepSeekBot Connect 的 relay 会解除所有登录这个账号的电脑的关联，删除它们的隧道和地址，再删除账号本身（[#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)）。
