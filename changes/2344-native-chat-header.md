## en

- The mobile chat uses the system navigation header. On iOS it has no bar of its own: a round Liquid Glass back button, then a separate glass capsule with the Bot's avatar and name that opens its profile, and a round button on the right for the Bot's computer; messages scrolling under it soften with the system scroll edge effect. Android shows the same items in a Material top app bar. The message list now runs top to bottom, opens at the newest message and keeps its place while older history loads, the composer sits just above the keyboard without an extra gap, and dragging the messages puts the keyboard away. ([#2344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2344))

## zh

- 手机端聊天页改用系统导航栏。iOS 上导航栏没有底色：左侧是圆形液态玻璃返回按钮，紧挨着一枚独立的玻璃胶囊，显示 Bot 头像和名字，点按打开 Bot 资料；右侧一个圆形按钮打开 Bot 的电脑；消息滚到导航栏下方时由系统边缘效果柔和淡出。Android 在 Material 顶部应用栏中显示同样的内容。消息列表改为自上而下排列，打开时停在最新消息，加载更早的历史时当前位置保持不动；键盘弹出时输入框紧贴键盘上方，不再留出多余空隙；拖动消息即可收起键盘。([#2344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2344))
