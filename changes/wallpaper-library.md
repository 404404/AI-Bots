## en

- Appearance settings are redesigned: theme and color cards with previews, a wallpaper library you can click or drop several images into, automatic switching with an optional shuffle, and clearer spacing for restoring defaults. The first wallpaper also makes untuned panels see-through ([#2362](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2362)).

## zh

- 重新设计界面设置：主题和配色改为带预览的卡片；壁纸改为壁纸库，可点击或拖入多张图片，支持自动切换和随机顺序；恢复默认外观的按钮与说明拉开间距。首次设置壁纸时，未调整过的面板会自动变为半透明（[#2362](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2362)）。
