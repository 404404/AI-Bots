## en

- Android computer menus, unpair confirmations, creation choices and group confirmations now use rounded, themed app dialogs consistent with Settings. Cancel and Back leave data unchanged; pending destructive actions cannot be submitted twice, and failed actions stay available for retry ([#2106](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2106)).

## zh

- Android 的电脑菜单、取消配对、新建选项和群组确认框统一采用与设置页一致的圆角主题弹窗；取消和返回不会更改数据，执行中的危险操作不能重复提交，失败后可重试（[#2106](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2106)）。
