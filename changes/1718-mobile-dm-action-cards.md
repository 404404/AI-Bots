## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a Tool approval or a Human question now appears as a card in the PersonaBot's DM and is decided there: Allow once, Always allow or Deny, with Details showing the tool, folder and input, or lettered choices and Submit. A decided card collapses to one line and says when another device decided first or the request expired, and opening the request from Activity or a notification jumps to its card ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- 在 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 中，工具审批和正式提问现在以卡片形式出现在 PersonaBot 的私信里，并可直接在私信中处理：允许一次、始终允许或拒绝，「详情」可查看工具、目录和输入；提问则以字母选项作答后提交。处理过的卡片收起为一行，并会说明是否已在另一台设备上处理或已过期；从「动态」或通知打开请求时会直接跳到对应卡片（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
