## en

- On Android, the workspace drawer follows the Material 3 navigation drawer: pill-shaped rows, a highlighted current Host or workspace, and a width that always leaves room to tap outside; connection addresses no longer break inside the port at large text ([#2351](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2351)).

## zh

- Android 上的环境切换抽屉改成 Material 3 导航抽屉：药丸形行、当前 Host 或工作区高亮，宽度始终留出可点击关闭的空白；大字号下连接地址不再在端口中间折行（[#2351](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2351)）。
