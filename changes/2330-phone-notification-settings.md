## en

- The phone app's notification settings get an **Allow notifications** switch per computer, and each DM and group gets **Do Not Disturb on this phone** (mentions and replies stop notifying this phone; tool approvals, questions and private input still do) plus **Mute on this computer**. A PersonaBot's replies in a DM now notify the phone ([#2330](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2330), [#2328](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2328)).

## zh

- 手机应用的通知设置新增按电脑的「允许通知」总开关；每个 DM 和群组新增「这台手机免打扰」（这里的提及和回复不再通知这台手机，工具审批、提问和私密输入照常通知）和「在这台电脑上静音」。PersonaBot 在 DM 里的回复现在会推送到手机（[#2330](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2330)、[#2328](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2328)）。
