## en

- Drag files anywhere onto a conversation to attach them; a drop target appears while files hover ([#1725](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1725)).
- Settings → General → Send messages chooses between ↵ and ⌘ ↵ (Ctrl ↵ on Windows and Linux) to send, shown as keycaps; the other key inserts a new line ([#1726](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1726)).
- New composer slash commands: `/memory` and `/sessions` in a DM, plus `/profile`, `/settings` and `/sidebar` in DMs and Group Channels ([#1727](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1727)).
- Your own messages that use Markdown (code blocks, inline code, bold, lists, headings, quotes, links, tables) now render formatted, keeping the line breaks you typed ([#1728](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1728)).

## zh

- 把文件拖到会话任意位置即可添加附件，拖动时会出现放置区域（[#1725](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1725)）。
- 设置 → 通用 → 发送消息 以键帽显示，可选 ↵ 或 ⌘ ↵（Windows 和 Linux 上为 Ctrl ↵）发送，另一种组合用于换行（[#1726](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1726)）。
- 输入框新增 / 命令：私聊里的 `/memory`、`/sessions`，以及私聊和群聊都有的 `/profile`、`/settings`、`/sidebar`（[#1727](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1727)）。
- 你发的消息如果用了 Markdown（代码块、行内代码、粗体、列表、标题、引用、链接、表格），现在会按格式显示，并保留你输入的换行（[#1728](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1728)）。
