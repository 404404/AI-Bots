## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, tapping the Bot's name at the top of a DM opens its profile: avatar, name, Bot Tags and Bot Bio, with Message, Computer and Notifications buttons. Below them you can read the Bot's Soul, its Core Memory and Memory files, its schedules, the group Channels it is in and the folders shared with it. Editing stays on the computer for now ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里，点私聊顶部的 Bot 名字会打开它的资料页：头像、名字、Bot 标签和 Bot 简介，以及「发消息」「电脑」「通知」按钮。下面可以查看 Bot 的 Soul、核心记忆和记忆文件、定时任务、所在的群聊频道和共享给它的文件夹。暂时只能查看，编辑仍在电脑上完成（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
