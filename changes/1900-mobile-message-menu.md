## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a held message bubble now shows it can be pressed (a ripple on Android, a slight lift on iOS), and a long-press opens the message's menu right at the bubble instead of a sheet from the bottom: a rounded reaction bar that scrolls sideways above it, and Copy and Share below it ([#1900](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1900)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里按住消息气泡时会有按压反馈（Android 为波纹，iOS 为轻微收缩），长按后菜单直接在气泡旁弹出，不再从底部弹出面板：上方是可横向滚动的圆角回应条，下方是复制和分享（[#1900](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1900)）。
