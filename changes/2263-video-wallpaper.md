## en

- DeepSeekBot now accepts device-local video wallpapers up to 100 MB, with the same crop and persistence as images. Videos loop silently without controls, pause in the background, and show a saved poster with a reason in settings when playback fails; the targeted CLI reports actual playback state ([#2263](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2263)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 支持最大 100 MB 的本机视频壁纸，复用图片的取景和保存路径。视频静音循环、无控件，后台暂停；播放失败显示已保存封面，并在设置中说明原因，定向 CLI 可读取实际播放状态（[#2263](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2263)）。
