## en

- Long-pressing a message in the mobile app now opens a native bottom sheet, like Slack and Grok: a row of reactions under the grabber, then Copy, Share and any Open action with a leading icon, instead of a menu drawn next to the bubble ([#2352](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2352)).

## zh

- 在手机端长按消息，现在会打开原生底部抽屉，参照 Slack 和 Grok：拖动条下方是一排回应表情，下面是带前置图标的复制、分享和打开操作，不再在气泡旁边弹出自绘菜单（[#2352](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2352)）。
