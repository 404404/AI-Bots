## en

- The Bot mode sidebar now shows how many unread Bot messages each DM and group has. Right-click a conversation for **Mark as read** or **Mute**, or pick **Mark all as read** from the sidebar's **⋯** menu; a muted conversation turns grey and stays out of the Activity unread total. Hiding or moving conversations shows a toast with **Undo**. Alt+↑/↓ moves to the previous or next conversation and Alt+Shift+↑/↓ jumps to the previous or next unread one. `deepseekbot channel-unread` and `deepseekbot channel-mute` do the same from the CLI ([#1738](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1738), [#1739](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1739), [#1741](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1741), [#1742](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1742), [#1743](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1743)).

## zh

- Bot 模式侧边栏现在会在每个私聊和群聊上显示 Bot 未读消息数。右键会话可以**标为已读**或设为**免打扰**，侧边栏 **⋯** 菜单里可以**全部标为已读**；免打扰的会话变灰，未读不计入活动中心的总数。隐藏或移动会话后会弹出带**撤销**的提示。Alt+↑/↓ 切到上一个或下一个会话，Alt+Shift+↑/↓ 跳到上一个或下一个有未读的会话。CLI 里用 `deepseekbot channel-unread` 和 `deepseekbot channel-mute` 做同样的事（[#1738](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1738), [#1739](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1739), [#1741](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1741), [#1742](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1742), [#1743](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1743)）。
