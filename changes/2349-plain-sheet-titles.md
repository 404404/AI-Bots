section: Changed

## en

- On the phone, sheets such as Settings show a plain centred title instead of a glass capsule, with simple round close, back and confirm buttons, and second-level pages put their back button where the close button sits. Two- or three-way switches, such as the chat background type, are now the system's own segmented control with one-word labels that never wrap. In chats on iOS 26, messages now stay readable up to the header and composer and only blur away right at the screen edge, without a mismatched tint ([#2349](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2349)).

## zh

- 手机端的设置等 sheet 改为简洁的居中标题，不再有玻璃胶囊，关闭、返回和确认都是简单的圆形按钮，二级页面的返回按钮与关闭按钮位置一致。聊天背景类型等二选一、三选一的切换改用系统原生分段控件，每段只有一个词，不会再换行。iOS 26 聊天页的消息在顶栏和输入框附近保持清晰，只在屏幕最边缘渐隐模糊，不再有颜色不搭的遮罩（[#2349](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2349)）。
