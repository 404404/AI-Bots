## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, each Bot's messages in a group Channel now show the Bot's avatar beside them, like Slack and the Web Client; the avatar rests at the bottom of the Bot's last bubble and sticks to the bottom edge while a long group scrolls past, so it is clear at a glance who is speaking ([#1890](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1890)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的群聊频道里，每个 Bot 的消息旁边现在会像 Slack 和 Web 端一样显示它的头像：头像停在该 Bot 最后一条消息的底部，滚动经过较长的一组消息时会贴住底边跟随，一眼就能看出是谁在说话（[#1890](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1890)）。
