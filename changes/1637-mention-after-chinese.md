## en

- Typing @ straight after Chinese text or punctuation such as 。 now opens the Bot picker, so a message can mention several Bots without a space before each @ ([#1637](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1637)).

## zh

- 在中文或“。”等标点后面直接输入 @ 现在会弹出 Bot 列表，一条消息里可以连续 @ 多个 Bot，不用先打空格（[#1637](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1637)）。
