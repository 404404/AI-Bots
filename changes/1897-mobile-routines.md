## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, tapping a routine in a PersonaBot's profile opens it: run it now, pause or resume it, delete it, and see its recent runs. **New routine** and **Edit** set a title, an instruction and a daily, weekly or every-few-hours schedule, with a preview of the next runs; other schedules open the full client ([#1897](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1897)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里，点 PersonaBot 资料页的例行任务即可打开它：立即运行、暂停或恢复、删除，并查看最近的运行记录。**新建例行任务**和**编辑**可以设置标题、指令，以及每天、每周或每隔几小时的时间规则，并预览接下来的运行时间；其他时间规则会打开完整客户端（[#1897](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1897)）。
