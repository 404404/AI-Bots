## en

- On desktop, Bot mode now has its own left sidebar with the Activity Center, the Messages list, Bot settings and an **Exit Bot mode** button. The sidebar can still be resized and collapsed to a rail. Other plugins' items in the left sidebar are hidden while Bot mode is on and come back when you exit ([#1790](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1790)).

## zh

- 桌面端 Bot 模式现在有自己的左侧栏：活动中心、消息列表、Bot 设置和 **退出 Bot 模式** 按钮。侧栏仍可拖动调整宽度，也可以收起成窄栏。Bot 模式期间，其他插件放在左侧栏的入口会暂时隐藏，退出后恢复（[#1790](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1790)）。
