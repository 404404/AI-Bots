## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now shows how many tokens the current computer used in the last 7 days: Settings, opened from your avatar in Chats, starts with a Usage card with the week's total and a bar per day, and tapping it shows today or the week split by kind and by PersonaBot, plus each PersonaBot's Memory changes ([#1899](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1899)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 现在能看到当前电脑最近 7 天用了多少 tokens：从聊天列表右上角的头像打开设置，最上面是用量卡片，显示这一周的总量和每天一根柱子；点进去可以切换今天或最近 7 天，按类型、按 PersonaBot 查看明细，还能看到每个 PersonaBot 记忆的变化（[#1899](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1899)）。
