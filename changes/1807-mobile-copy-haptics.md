## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, long-press a DM message to copy its whole text, and the phone gives a light tap of feedback when you send a message, allow or deny a Tool approval, or submit an answer. Chat photos now stay cached on the phone, so they reopen offline and no longer flash an earlier photo while you scroll ([#1807](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1807)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的私聊里，长按一条消息即可复制全文；发送消息、允许或拒绝工具审批、提交回答时，手机会有轻微的触感反馈。聊天图片现在会缓存在手机上，离线也能再次打开，滚动时也不会先闪出别的图片（[#1807](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1807)）。
