## en

- If a computer couldn't be reached when the DeepSeekBot mobile app opened, the app now finishes turning on its notifications as soon as that computer is reachable again, or when you return to the app. Before, Notifications kept saying the phone wasn't registered with that computer until you quit and reopened the app ([#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 打开时如果连不上某台电脑，现在等那台电脑重新连上，或你回到 App 时，App 会自动为它开启通知。以前「通知」页会一直提示还没能把这台手机告诉那台电脑，要彻底退出 App 再打开才行（[#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)）。
