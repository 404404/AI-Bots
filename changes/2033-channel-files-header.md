## en

- Open Channel files from the Messages header in DMs and Groups to browse and reference files shared by Humans and all Bots, including older messages; the compact list opens on demand inside Messages instead of occupying a separate Dock panel, with icon actions aligned right and wrapping only when needed ([#2033](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2033)).

## zh

- 单聊和群聊均可从聊天面板 header 打开「频道文件」，查阅并引用 Human 与所有 Bot 共享的文件，包括较早消息；紧凑列表在聊天内按需展开，不再单独占用 Dock 面板；图标操作靠右排列，宽度不足时换行（[#2033](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2033)）。
