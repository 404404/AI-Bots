## en

- With an Approval Template selected, the Approval Reviewer now also answers `secret_cli` and `secret_mcp_connect` calls and Computer and Browser Authorization, after saved rules and before the card. It sees a secret tool's program, arguments, account and placeholder names, never a secret value, and the folded record shows the same masked input. "Cautious" sends these to you; "Get the work done" allows a known program with the account for its own service, and Browser Authorization when the task needs it ([#1827](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1827)).

## zh

- 选中自动审批模板后，审批员现在也会回答 `secret_cli`、`secret_mcp_connect` 调用以及电脑和浏览器授权，顺序在已保存规则之后、审批卡片之前。对密钥工具，它只看到程序、参数、账号和占位符名称，看不到任何密钥值，折叠记录里也只显示同样脱敏后的输入。“谨慎”模板把这些交给你；“把活干完”模板允许已知程序配合它自己服务的账号，并在任务需要网页时允许浏览器授权（[#1827](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1827)）。
