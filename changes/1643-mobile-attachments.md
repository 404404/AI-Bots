## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, the **+** in a DM's composer now sends photos, camera shots and files to the PersonaBot. Picked files show as removable tiles in the composer, and a failed send retries without uploading finished files again ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊输入框的 **+** 现在可以把相册照片、现拍的照片和文件发给 PersonaBot。选中的文件以可移除的缩略图显示在输入框里；发送失败后重试时，已上传完的文件不会重复上传（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
