## en

- The mobile app's Computer screen has no take-control button any more: the first drag, tap, button or key pauses the Bot and is sent, and the Bot resumes 30 seconds after your last input, or at once from the hand-back icon. The password keyboard keeps the Bot paused until you put it away. Typing from the phone into a Chromium window on a Linux Computer desktop now arrives instead of being refused, and hiding the keyboard returns to Mouse ([#1865](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1865)).

## zh

- 手机 App 的 Computer 界面去掉了接管按钮：第一次拖动、点按、按键就会暂停 Bot 并把操作发出去，最后一次操作 30 秒后 Bot 自动恢复，也可以点交还图标立即交还。密码键盘打开期间 Bot 一直暂停。在手机上向 Linux Computer 桌面里的 Chromium 窗口打字现在能送达，不再被拒绝；收起键盘会回到鼠标模式（[#1865](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1865)）。
