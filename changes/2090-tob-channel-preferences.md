## en

- Tob can mute, pin, hide or restore a Channel and set the Human's Channel nickname through reviewed CLI commands. Changes preserve other Channels' preferences and reject stale state or revoked access ([#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-channel-personal-preferences)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过经审核的 CLI 命令设置频道免打扰、置顶、隐藏或恢复，并修改 Human 在该频道的昵称。操作保留其他频道偏好，拒绝过期状态与已撤销权限（[#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-channel-personal-preferences)）。
