## en

- Tob can start, inspect and cancel qualified IM setup through owned opaque receipts; the Human continues the same attempt in Applications using the existing private form, without giving QR or credentials to Tob or starting a second setup ([#2094](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2094)).

- Tob can review Workspace grants, Bot access, Approval Templates/rules/reviewer settings and existing connector policies through the CLI and their current owners, with fresh Human decisions, stale-state refusal and canonical readback; Browser pairing also gains explicit Human CLI commands while private credentials and OS consent keep their existing flows ([#2094](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2094), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-grants-approval-and-connector-policies)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过所属的不透明收据启动、查询、取消合格 IM 配置；Human 在“应用”中接续同一尝试并使用原有私密表单，二维码和凭据不交给 Tob，也不重复启动配置（[#2094](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2094)）。

- Tob 可通过 CLI 与现有 owner 审阅工作区授权、Bot 访问权限、审批模板／规则／审核模型及已有连接器策略；每次修改需 Human 新作决定，拒绝过期状态并规范读回。浏览器配对补充显式 Human CLI 命令，私密凭据与系统授权继续走原流程（[#2094](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2094)、[CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.zh.md#tob-授权审批与连接器策略)）。
