## en

- The `deepseekbot` CLI can now work the Human Inbox and its related controls on a live Host: list, count, dismiss and ignore Inbox items, read an assignment report's context, list a group Channel's assignment work, move a Channel between sidebar sections, list Bot Sessions and their owner, archive assignment Sessions, list and revoke "always allow" tool approval rules, revoke a Workspace Grant or change its write access, and turn on or remove a private MCP server. Revokes and removals need `--confirm` ([#1699](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1699)).

## zh

- `deepseekbot` 命令行现在可以在在线 Host 上处理 Human Inbox 及相关控制：列出、统计、忽略和关闭 Inbox 条目，读取委派汇报的上下文，列出群组 Channel 的委派工作，在侧栏分组之间移动 Channel，列出 Bot 的会话及其归属，归档委派会话，列出和撤销"始终允许"的工具审批规则，撤销工作区授权或修改其写入权限，以及开关或移除私有 MCP 服务器。撤销和移除操作需要 `--confirm`（[#1699](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1699)）。
