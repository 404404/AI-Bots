## en

- The Channel sidebar, its Sessions, Work, Bot Inbox and Memory tabs, the conversation header, the composer context bar, the mobile Bot panel and conversation header, the desktop window companion, and the Computer and Browser settings now take their spacing, type, radius, size and colour from one set of design tokens. They look as before, with small alignments to the shared scale: a few gaps and paddings move by 2px, small labels go from 11px to 12px, focus rings use the accent colour, and disabled buttons are dimmed ([#1602](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1602)).

## zh

- 频道侧栏及其会话、工作、Bot 收件箱和记忆标签页、会话标题栏、输入框上方的上下文栏、移动端 Bot 面板与会话标题栏、桌面窗口伴侣，以及 Computer 和 Browser 设置，现在统一从同一套设计令牌取间距、字号、圆角、尺寸和颜色。外观与之前一致，只做了少量对齐：部分间距和内边距调整 2px，小号文字从 11px 调到 12px，焦点框使用强调色，禁用按钮变暗（[#1602](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1602)）。
