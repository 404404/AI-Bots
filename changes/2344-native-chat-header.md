## en

- The mobile chat uses the system navigation header: on iOS a transparent header whose scroll edge effect softens messages beneath it, on Android a Material top app bar. The message list now runs top to bottom, opens at the newest message and keeps its place while older history loads. ([#2344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2344))

## zh

- 手机端聊天页改用系统导航栏：iOS 上是透明导航栏，消息滚到下面时由系统边缘效果柔和淡出；Android 上是 Material 顶部应用栏。消息列表改为自上而下排列，打开时停在最新消息，加载更早的历史时当前位置保持不动。([#2344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2344))
