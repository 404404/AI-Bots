## en

- In Bot mode, Ctrl+K (⌘K on macOS) opens a quick switcher: type part of a PersonaBot, group chat or channel name — Chinese names included — then use the arrow keys and Enter to open it the same way its sidebar row does. An empty search lists recent conversations, hidden channels rank last, and the shortcut also works while typing in the composer ([#1740](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1740)).

## zh

- Bot 模式下按 Ctrl+K（macOS 为 ⌘K）可打开快速切换：输入 PersonaBot、群聊或频道名称的一部分（支持中文名），用方向键选择、回车打开，效果与点击侧边栏对应行相同。搜索为空时列出最近的会话，隐藏的频道排在最后；在输入框里打字时也可使用该快捷键（[#1740](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1740)）。
