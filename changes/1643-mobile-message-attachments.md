## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, DM messages now show their photos as thumbnails and other files as rows with a name and size, for your own messages, PersonaBot replies and bridged IM media. A photo you send appears right away. Tap a photo to view it full screen, where you can pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag to look around and swipe down to close; tap a file to download it from your computer and share it ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的私聊消息现在会直接显示附件：图片显示为缩略图，其他文件显示为带名称和大小的一行，你自己发的消息、PersonaBot 的回复和桥接进来的 IM 媒体都一样。刚发出的照片会立即出现。点图片可全屏查看，支持双指或双击缩放、拖动查看细节、下滑关闭；点文件会从你的电脑下载并打开系统分享（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
