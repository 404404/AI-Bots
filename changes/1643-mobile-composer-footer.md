## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a DM's composer now has a footer with the PersonaBot's model and its folder count, like the Web Client. Tapping the model switches the Bot's main model or applies a preset; tapping the folders adds or removes a folder after a scoped confirmation, turns Full access on or off behind its danger confirmation with the chat or Assignments scope, and picks the auto approval template ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊输入框下方新增一行，和网页客户端一样显示 PersonaBot 的模型和已授权的文件夹数。点模型可以切换 Bot 的主模型或套用预设；点文件夹可以在确认授权范围后添加文件夹、移除文件夹，在危险确认后开关完全访问并选择聊天会话或仅新事项的范围，以及选择自动审批模板（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
