## en

- Global Settings can choose multiple types for the Activity navigation number, defaulting to pending tool approvals only; the choice is saved on this device ([#2077](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2077)).

- Android's Chats and Activity navigation floats in a compact rounded rectangle with centered icon/label groups, a compact tonal selected icon tile and a faint shadow. Inactive Activity replaces its icon with a tabular count when there are pending items, with a smooth transition back to its icon on selection; the bar expands with system text size and keeps scroll hide/reveal behavior ([#2077](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2077)).

## zh

- 全局设置可多选动态数字包含的类型，默认只统计等待处理的工具审批，并在这台设备保存选择（[#2077](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2077)）。

- Android 的聊天与动态导航改为紧凑的圆角矩形浮岛，图标与文字垂直居中，选中图标使用紧凑的强调色底块，阴影更淡。聊天页有待办时，动态图标平滑切换为等宽数字；选中动态后恢复图标；底栏随系统字号扩展，并保留滚动时隐藏与显示的交互（[#2077](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2077)）。
