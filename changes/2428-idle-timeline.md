## en

- Fixed Bot mode lagging while idle on long conversations since 1.4.0: hidden hover toolbars no longer make every repaint rebuild each bubble, onboarding polls no longer re-render the conversation, and message times and fold sizes are measured more cheaply ([#2413](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2413)).

## zh

- 修复自 1.4.0 起 Bot 模式在长对话中空闲时卡顿的问题：隐藏的悬浮工具栏不再让每次重绘重建所有气泡，引导轮询不再重渲染整个对话，消息时间与折叠尺寸的计算也更省（[#2413](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2413)）。
