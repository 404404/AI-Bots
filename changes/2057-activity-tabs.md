## en

- Mobile Activity now opens on Needs you, with three equal-width tabs for pending work, personal updates and other unread conversations. Swipe the full page to switch while retaining each list's position; the selected icon and label are centered together above a full-width underline that follows the gesture. All and handled history stay in filters ([#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)).
- Tab icons now fade and scale with the page gesture, while their labels shift smoothly into the centered icon/label group; cancelling the drag reverses the same transition ([#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)).

- Activity now keeps only its tabs visible while browsing downward, with no top fade; Activity and Chats hide their bottom navigation on downward scrolling and restore it on upward travel. Search/filter controls stay visible while in use ([#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)).

## zh

- 移动端 Activity 默认打开「待你处理」，三个等宽 tab 分别显示待办、与你有关的动态和其他未读。支持整页左右滑动切换并保留各自列表位置；选中项的图标与文字整体居中，下沿线铺满单个 tab 并跟随手势移动。「全部」及「已处理」保留在筛选里（[#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)）。
- Tab 图标的淡入与缩放随页面手势连续变化，文字平滑移动到图标和文字整体居中的位置；取消拖动时，沿同一进度自然退回（[#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)）。

- 向下浏览时，Activity 收起大标题和操作按钮，只保留三个 tab，并移除列表顶部渐隐；Activity 和聊天列表的底部导航随滚动隐藏，向上回看时恢复。搜索和筛选期间保持操作可见（[#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)）。
