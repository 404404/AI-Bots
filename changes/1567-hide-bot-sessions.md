## en

- Bot settings › General › Show Bot sessions in the session list controls whether Bot Orchestrator and Assignment sessions appear in DeepSeek Harness's own session list. It is on by default; turn it off to hide them ([#1567](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1567)).

## zh

- 新增 Bot 设置 › 通用 › 在会话列表显示 Bot 会话，用来控制 Bot 的 Orchestrator 和 Assignment 会话是否出现在 DeepSeek Harness 自带的会话列表中。默认开启；关闭后这些会话会被隐藏（[#1567](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1567)）。
