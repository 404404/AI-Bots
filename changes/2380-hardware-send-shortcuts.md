## en

- With a hardware keyboard, ⌘↩ sends a message on iPhone and iPad, and Enter sends on Android (Shift+Enter adds a line). A long-press on the app icon now lists the three most recently opened chats ([#2380](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2380)).

## zh

- 接上实体键盘后，iPhone 和 iPad 上按 ⌘↩ 发送消息，Android 上按回车发送（Shift+回车换行）。长按 App 图标会列出最近打开的三个聊天，点一下直接进入（[#2380](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2380)）。
