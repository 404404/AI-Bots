## en

- On desktop, Bot mode now has its own left sidebar headed **DeepSeekBot**, with the Activity Center, the Messages list and Bot settings. Hover the logo at the top left and it turns into **Exit Bot mode**. The sidebar can still be resized and collapsed to a rail. Other plugins' items in the left sidebar are hidden while Bot mode is on and come back when you exit ([#1790](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1790)).

## zh

- 桌面端 Bot 模式现在有自己的左侧栏，标题为 **DeepSeekBot**，包含活动中心、消息列表和 Bot 设置。鼠标移到左上角的 Logo 上，它会变成 **退出 Bot 模式** 按钮。侧栏仍可拖动调整宽度，也可以收起成窄栏。Bot 模式期间，其他插件放在左侧栏的入口会暂时隐藏，退出后恢复（[#1790](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1790)）。
