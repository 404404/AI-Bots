# Community conduct / 社区行为规范

## English

This policy applies to issues, discussions, PRs and project-managed community spaces. All backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

- Be respectful. Discuss ideas and behavior; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks.
- Help newcomers find the right place. A mistaken category or incomplete report is not misconduct.
- Stay relevant; do not spam, impersonate others or post private information without permission.
- Give credit, including for reports, testing, documentation and answers.

Maintainers moderate the community. They may request corrections, hide or remove harmful content, or restrict participation for serious or repeated violations. Where safe, explain the reason and allow clarification; enforcement should be proportionate and consistent.

Ask a maintainer in the relevant thread about ordinary moderation. For sensitive complaints, request a private contact route without details and wait for confirmation. No private reporting channel is currently published; a public request is not confidential. Do not publicly post someone else's personal information or sensitive complaint evidence.

## 中文

本规范适用于 Issue、Discussion、PR 和项目管理的社区空间。欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者。

- 尊重彼此，讨论观点和行为，不骚扰、歧视、威胁或做个人攻击。
- 帮新人找到正确入口；分类错误、报告不完整不等于违规。
- 保持相关，不刷屏、不冒充他人，不未经许可公开私人信息。
- 认可反馈、测试、文档、答疑等所有贡献。

维护者负责治理，可以要求纠正、隐藏或移除有害内容，对严重或反复违规限制参与。在安全的前提下说明原因并允许澄清，处理应适度且一致。

一般治理问题可以在相关帖询问维护者。敏感投诉先只请求私下渠道，等待确认再发送细节。目前尚未公布私密举报渠道；公开请求不是保密报告。不要公开他人的个人信息或敏感投诉证据。
