## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a DM's header now has no background, so messages scroll under it. Back sits on the left, and a round chip in the middle shows the Bot's avatar, its name and a dot for its state: idle, working, waiting for you or blocked ([#1875](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1875)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊页的顶栏不再有背景，消息可以滚到它下面。左侧是返回按钮，中间是一个圆角胶囊，依次显示 Bot 的头像、名字和表示状态的小圆点：空闲、工作中、等你处理或受阻（[#1875](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1875)）。
