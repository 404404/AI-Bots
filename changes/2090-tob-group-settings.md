## en

- Tob can change group names, avatars and member wake policies, or remove Bot members through the CLI with existing approval and permission checks. Reviewed snapshots reject concurrent changes; removal keeps the Bot and group history ([#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090), [CLI guide](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-settings-and-member-removal)).

## zh

- Tob 可通过 CLI 修改群名称、头像和成员唤醒策略，或移除 Bot 成员，沿用现有审批与权限校验。审核快照防止覆盖并发修改，移除成员保留 Bot 与群历史（[#2090](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2090)、[CLI 指南](../docs/bot-cli.md#tob-group-settings-and-member-removal)）。
