## en

- Activity Center now opens the personal Inbox directly on the page and in Dock, filling the available Panel width with cards styled like the Channel list, matching Panel insets and independently collapsible Needs you, For you and Other unread sections, plus header search across eligible results and a menu for filters, history and sorting, with inline Tool approval buttons, expandable context, retryable failures, and batch actions behind an explicit selection mode. Retired Overview navigation, state, styles and duplicate legacy UI tests are removed; open reply drafts survive read-state refreshes ([#2026](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2026)).
