## en

- The quick switcher now opens with Alt+K (Option+K on macOS), which the desktop app no longer swallows; Ctrl+K and Command+K still work where the browser delivers them. It also finds Bot settings down to the individual row on each settings page and opens that row highlighted, matches Chinese names by full pinyin or initials, and accepts English words in the Chinese interface ([#2360](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2360)).

## zh

- 快速搜索改用 Alt+K（macOS 为 Option+K）打开，桌面端不再吞掉这个快捷键；浏览器能收到 Ctrl+K 和 Command+K 时也照常可用。搜索现在能找到 Bot 设置，精确到每个设置页里的具体一行并高亮打开，中文名称可用全拼或首字母命中，中文界面下也能用英文词过滤（[#2360](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2360)）。
