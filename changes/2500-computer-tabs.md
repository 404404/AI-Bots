## en

- The channel list has Computer tabs: **All** (the default), **This computer**, and one tab per other Computer. **Add computer** takes the pairing link from another Computer's Settings → Devices → Pair a phone, so a browser can list, open and answer that Computer's chats over Tailscale. Right-click a Computer's tab to remove it ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).

## zh

- 频道列表加了电脑 tabs：**全部**（默认）、**这台电脑**，以及每台其他电脑各一个。**添加电脑**里粘贴另一台电脑「设置 → 设备 → 配对手机」的配对链接，浏览器就能通过 Tailscale 列出、打开并回复那台电脑的聊天。右键电脑的 tab 可以移除它（[#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)）。
