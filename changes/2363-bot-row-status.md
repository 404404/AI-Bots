## en

- Bot rows in the conversation list now show one clear status: a spinner while the Bot works, an amber ring when it needs you, a red mark when it is blocked, and a check when it has finished. Avatars use a rounded square with the unread count on their corner, section headers say how many Bots need you, and times follow the usual chat style (time today, Yesterday, weekday, then date). The phone app shows the same glyph at the end of the preview line ([#2363](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2363)).

## zh

- 会话列表里每个 Bot 只显示一个清晰的状态：工作中转圈，需要你时琥珀色圆环，受阻时红色标记，做完后显示勾。头像改为圆角矩形，未读数放在头像右上角；分组标题显示有几个 Bot 需要你；时间按聊天软件的习惯显示（今天显示钟点，然后是“昨天”、星期几、日期）。手机端在预览行末尾显示同样的状态图标（[#2363](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2363)）。
