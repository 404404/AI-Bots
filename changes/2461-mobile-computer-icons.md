## en

- Mobile: **Customize computer** now picks an icon (native symbols or emoji) and a colour instead of a header banner and avatar. The icon leads that computer's tab above Chats and its drawer tile, and the colour tints the icon and the tab underline. The custom header colour had stopped showing since the native header; saved banner colours carry over as the nearest preset, while old avatars and pictures are removed ([#2461](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2461)).

## zh

- 移动端：**自定义电脑**改为选择图标（系统图标或 emoji）和颜色，不再设置顶栏背景和头像。图标显示在「聊天」上方该电脑标签的最左侧和电脑抽屉里，颜色用于图标和标签下划线。改用系统原生 header 后自定义顶栏颜色已不再显示；已保存的顶栏颜色会换成最接近的预设色，旧头像和图片会被移除（[#2461](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2461)）。
