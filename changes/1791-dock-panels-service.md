## en

- Other plugins can now add their own panels to the desktop Bot mode layout through the `dockPanels` Service. A panel either follows the active Channel or stays the same across Channels, and it appears in the Panels menu next to the built-in ones ([#1791](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1791)).

## zh

- 其他插件现在可以通过 `dockPanels` Service 往桌面 Bot 模式布局里添加自己的面板。面板可以跟随当前 Channel，也可以在切换 Channel 时保持不变，并和内置面板一起出现在「面板」菜单中（[#1791](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1791)）。
