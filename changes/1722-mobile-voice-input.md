## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, the mic in a DM's composer now turns speech into text on the phone. The words fill the message field as you speak, so you can edit them before sending; **Stop** keeps the text and **×** cancels. If the microphone or speech recognition is not allowed, the chat says so ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 私聊输入框的麦克风现在可以在手机上把语音转成文字。说话时文字会填进输入框，发送前可以修改；点**停止**保留文字，点 **×** 取消。如果没有允许麦克风或语音识别，聊天里会给出提示（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
