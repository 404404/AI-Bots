## en

- Channel settings are easier to find: right-click a Channel in the Messages sidebar for Channel settings, My nickname and the DM's "Show what this Bot did elsewhere" toggles, or click the conversation header chip for the Profile and the same options. The ⋯ button at the top right of the conversation is gone ([#1761](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1761)).

## zh

- 频道设置更好找了：在消息侧边栏右键频道即可打开「频道设置」「我的昵称」以及私聊的「显示 Bot 在别处的动作」开关；点击对话顶部的名称胶囊也能打开资料和同样的选项。对话区右上角的 ⋯ 按钮已移除（[#1761](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1761)）。
