## en

- Create a PersonaBot or a Channel from the DeepSeekBot mobile app. On iOS, tap the compose button next to your avatar at the top of Chats; on Android, tap the + button at the bottom right. Pick New PersonaBot or New Channel and the full client opens on its create dialog ([#1854](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1854)).

## zh

- 在 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里就能新建 PersonaBot 或频道。iOS 上点聊天页顶部头像旁的新建按钮，Android 上点右下角的 + 按钮，选新建 PersonaBot 或新建频道，完整客户端会直接打开对应的创建对话框（[#1854](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1854)）。
