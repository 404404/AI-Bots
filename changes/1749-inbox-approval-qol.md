## en

- Tool approval cards are shorter and match the mobile app: a category (Terminal, File, Browser) with a **Needs you** pill, one sentence saying what the tool will do, a one-line command or file preview, then every choice as a borderless pill button: **Allow once**, **Always allow this exact call**, **Always allow all opaque tools in this scope** and a red **Reject**, with the full-access link below. Clicking the card shows the working directory, full input and risk note, and a decided card collapses to one line with a coloured dot, such as "Terminal · Allowed". They also take the keyboard: `Y` or Enter allows once, `N` or Esc rejects, and `A` opens the always-allow risk confirmation; a new card takes focus when nothing else has it. In the last hour the pill counts down to expiry and turns red in the last 15 minutes, and file writes and edits show a line diff instead of raw JSON, with long input folded ([#1749](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1749), [#1752](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1752), [#1753](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1753)). Question cards follow the same layout: the question's header (or **Question**) with a **Needs you** pill, the question, and lettered options. A single single-choice question answers on tap; several questions or multiple choice still end with **Answer and continue**. **Other answer** stays as a plain field, and an answered card collapses to "question · Answered".
- The Human Inbox moves with `J`/`K`, opens the source with Enter, marks an item handled with `E`, selects with `X` and snoozes with `S`. Selected items can be marked handled together, and selected tool approvals allowed once or rejected together. **Remind me later** hides an item until a chosen time; the **Snoozed** filter lists them and brings one back, and `deepseekbot inbox-snooze` does the same from the CLI ([#1750](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1750), [#1751](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1751), [#1754](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1754)).

## zh

- 工具审批卡更简洁，并与移动端一致：顶部是类别（终端、文件、浏览器）和「需要你确认」标签，接着一句话说明工具要做什么、一行命令或文件预览，然后所有选项都直接显示为无描边的胶囊按钮：「仅批准这一次」「始终允许相同调用」「始终允许此范围全部不透明工具」和红色的「拒绝」，下面是完全访问入口。点一下卡片就能看到工作目录、完整输入和风险说明；处理完的卡片收成带彩色圆点的一行，例如「终端 · 已允许」。卡片同时支持键盘：`Y` 或 Enter 仅批准这一次，`N` 或 Esc 拒绝，`A` 打开「始终允许」的风险确认；新卡片出现且页面没有其他焦点时会自动获得焦点。最后一小时标签改为显示还剩多久过期，最后 15 分钟变红；写文件和改文件的请求改为显示逐行 diff，不再是原始 JSON，过长的输入默认折叠（[#1749](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1749)、[#1752](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1752)、[#1753](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1753)）。提问卡片也用同样的布局：顶部是问题自带的标题（没有时写「提问」）和「需要你回答」标签，然后是问题和带字母的选项。只有一个单选问题时点一下就作答；多个问题或多选仍用「回答并继续」提交。「其他回答」保持为一个普通输入框；回答后卡片收成「问题 · 已回答」一行。
- Human Inbox 可以用 `J`/`K` 上下移动，Enter 跳到来源消息，`E` 标为已处理，`X` 选择，`S` 稍后提醒。选中的条目可以一起标为已处理，选中的工具审批可以一起批准一次或一起拒绝。「稍后提醒」把条目隐藏到选定的时间；「已延后」筛选列出它们并可取消延后，`deepseekbot inbox-snooze` 在命令行里做同样的事（[#1750](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1750)、[#1751](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1751)、[#1754](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1754)）。
