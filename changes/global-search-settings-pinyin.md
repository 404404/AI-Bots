## en

- The quick switcher now opens with Alt+K (Option+K on macOS), which the desktop app no longer swallows; Ctrl+K and Command+K still work where the browser delivers them. It also finds Bot settings, matches Chinese names by full pinyin or initials, and accepts English words in the Chinese interface.

## zh

- 快速搜索改用 Alt+K（macOS 为 Option+K）打开，桌面端不再吞掉这个快捷键；浏览器能收到 Ctrl+K 和 Command+K 时也照常可用。搜索现在能找到 Bot 设置项，中文名称可用全拼或首字母命中，中文界面下也能用英文词过滤。
