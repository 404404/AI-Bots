## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, each computer is customized from its row in the computer drawer: ⋯ (or a long press) → Customize sets its avatar, banner and a name shown only on the phone. Settings no longer has this entry ([#1878](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1878)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的电脑自定义改到左侧电脑抽屉里：点每台电脑的 ⋯（或长按）→“自定义”，可以设置头像、顶栏背景和只在手机上显示的名称。“设置”里不再有这个入口（[#1878](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1878)）。
