## en

- On iPhone and Android, the done checkmark at the end of a chat list row now always sits upright. It no longer keeps the tilted or upside-down angle the working spinner had when the Bot finished.

- iPhone 和 Android 上，会话列表每行末尾的完成打勾图标现在总是正向显示，不再沿用 Bot 完成时转圈指示器停下的角度而歪斜或倒置。
