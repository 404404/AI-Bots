## en

- Mobile Activity now opens on Needs you, with three equal-width tabs for pending work, personal updates and other unread conversations. Swipe the full page to switch while retaining each list's position; the selected icon and label are centered together above a full-width underline that follows the gesture. All and handled history stay in filters ([#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)).

## zh

- 移动端 Activity 默认打开「待你处理」，三个等宽 tab 分别显示待办、与你有关的动态和其他未读。支持整页左右滑动切换并保留各自列表位置；选中项的图标与文字整体居中，下沿线铺满单个 tab 并跟随手势移动。「全部」及「已处理」保留在筛选里（[#2057](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2057)）。
