## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, Tool approval and question cards are simpler. The first line names the kind of request with a "Needs you" tag, then one sentence says what is asked; the folder and full input sit under a Details toggle. A single-choice question is answered with one tap, and in a PersonaBot's DM a message typed in the chat answers the open question ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- 在 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 中，工具审批和提问卡片更简洁了：第一行写请求类别，并带「需要你确认」标签，下面一句话说明要你决定什么；目录和完整输入收进可展开的「详情」。单选提问点一下即可作答；在 PersonaBot 私信里，直接在聊天框发送的话会作为当前提问的回答（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
