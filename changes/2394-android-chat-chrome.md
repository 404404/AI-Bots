## en

- On Android, the chat header now floats over the messages like on iOS: a round Back, the Bot's avatar and name in their own rounded chip, and a round computer button. Messages fade out under the header and under the composer instead of the fade stopping above the composer, and the composer's + is a filled round button ([#2394](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2394)).

## zh

- Android 聊天页顶部改为像 iOS 一样悬浮在消息上：圆形返回按钮、Bot 头像和名称的圆角岛状 chip、右侧圆形按钮。消息在顶部栏和输入框下方渐隐，不再卡在输入框上方；输入框左侧的 + 改为带背景的圆形按钮（[#2394](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2394)）。
