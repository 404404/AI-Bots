## en

- **Hosted Connect is open to every signed-in account**: the relay no longer requires an allowlist. Any account that signs in to DeepSeekBot (for example with Google or an emailed code) can link up to five computers; an operator can still restrict it with `ALLOWED_USERS` ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).

## zh

- **托管 Connect 对所有登录账号开放**：relay 不再要求白名单。任何登录 DeepSeekBot 的账号（例如用 Google 或邮箱验证码登录）都能关联最多 5 台电脑；运营方仍可用 `ALLOWED_USERS` 限制（[#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)）。
