## en

- The conversation view, the Bot list, the workspace rail and the panels docked beside a Channel no longer re-render for writes they do not show. Human Inbox counts and search typing leave the conversation view still; a streamed reply no longer repaints the docked panels or the Bot list; an Activity frame that changes no visible state notifies nobody ([#2439](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2439)).

## zh

- 会话视图、Bot 列表、工作区控制栏和频道旁停靠的面板，不再因为与自己无关的写入而重新渲染。Human Inbox 计数和搜索输入不再让会话视图重绘；流式回复不再重绘停靠面板和 Bot 列表；没有改变可见状态的 Activity 帧不再通知任何订阅者（[#2439](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2439)）。
