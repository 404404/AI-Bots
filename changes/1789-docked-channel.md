## en

- On a desktop-width window, Bot mode lays the Channel body and each Channel sidebar entry out as separate panels you can drag beside each other, close and reopen; panels are never tabbed and the layout is kept in this browser ([#1789](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1789)).

## zh

- 在桌面宽度的窗口中，Bot 模式把 Channel 正文和每个 Channel sidebar 条目分成独立面板，可以拖到彼此旁边、关闭和重新打开；面板之间不会叠成标签页，布局保存在当前浏览器中（[#1789](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1789)）。
