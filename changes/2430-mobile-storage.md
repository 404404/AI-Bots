## en

- The phone app has **Settings → Storage**: see how much space chat media, messages and Memory use on your computer, pick an age, media types and chats, review what is deleted, and clear it. **Clear image cache** frees the images saved on the phone itself ([#2430](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2430)).

## zh

- 手机 App 新增**设置 → 存储空间**：查看电脑上聊天媒体、聊天记录和记忆的占用，选好时间、类型和聊天，确认要删除的内容后清理。**清除图片缓存**可以释放手机本身保存的图片（[#2430](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2430)）。
