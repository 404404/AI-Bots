## en

- Bot Inbox Panels now show edge-to-edge square event lists in both time and source views, including snoozed and completed groups, while preserving row spacing and controls ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

- Light-theme workspace gaps now use a subtly recessed background so Panels and the control rail remain distinct, matching the dark theme’s visual hierarchy ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

- Group Members opens beside Messages on the right with a persistent adjustable width; Channels uses Hash, Messages uses MessagesCircle and Sessions uses TextQuote, distinct from the two-person Members action ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

- Panel switch reordering now previews the insertion position and dims the dragged switch; dropping above or below a switch places it on the indicated side ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

- Switching Channels with Alt+digits or the roster now preserves the workspace and unrelated Panels, while resetting Channel-local transient UI state and retaining separate drafts ([#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)).

## zh

- Bot 收件箱面板的时间与来源视图统一采用贴边、无圆角的事件列表，涵盖稍后和已完成分组，并保留行内间距与操作控件（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。

- 浅色主题的工作区间隙改用略深的背景，使面板和控制列保持清晰、柔和的层次，与深色主题一致（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。

- 群聊成员区域从消息右侧展开，可调整并记住宽度；频道列表使用井字号，消息使用 MessagesCircle，会话使用 TextQuote，与成员管理的双人图标区分（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。

- 控制列图标排序新增插入位置提示与拖动态，拖到图标上方或下方即可放到对应位置（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。

- 使用 Alt＋数字或点击列表切换频道时保留工作区及无关面板，只重置频道内临时界面状态，草稿仍按频道独立保留（[#2017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2017)）。
