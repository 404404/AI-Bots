## en

- Hovering or keyboard-focusing a Bot's avatar or name in a DM or group conversation now opens a small profile card with its roles, description and current status (thinking, working on what, waiting or blocked), plus Message and Profile buttons; a Human author's card shows just the name. The card replaces the browser's plain tooltip ([#1744](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1744)).

## zh

- 在私聊或群聊中悬停或用键盘聚焦 Bot 的头像或名字时，会弹出一张小资料卡，显示它的角色、简介和当前状态（思考中、正在做什么、等待中或受阻），并提供「私聊」和「资料」按钮；Human 发送者的资料卡只显示名字。资料卡取代了浏览器原生的提示文字（[#1744](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1744)）。
