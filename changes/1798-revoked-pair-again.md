## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, when your computer revokes the phone while its Browser or Computer screen is open, the screen now offers **Pair again**. The DM you return to no longer stays on "Reconnecting to this DM…": it says your computer refused the app and offers **Pair again** too ([#1798](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1798)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里，打开 Bot 的浏览器或电脑界面时如果电脑撤销了这台手机，界面现在会提供**重新配对**。返回的私信也不会一直停在「正在重新连接这个私信…」，而是提示电脑拒绝了这个应用，并同样提供**重新配对**（[#1798](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1798)）。
