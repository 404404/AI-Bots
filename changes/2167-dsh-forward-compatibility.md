## en

- The 1.4.0 candidate admits DSH `>=0.2.0-rc.2 <0.3.0-0` instead of one exact RC, retaining the existing minimum through native Subagent, Owner identity and tool compatibility adapters, with pinned development dependencies and separate real-version qualification; future 0.2.x admission is not a claim of runtime verification ([#2167](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2167)).

## zh

- 1.4.0 候选版将 DSH 安装范围改为 `>=0.2.0-rc.2 <0.3.0-0`，不再锁死单个 RC；保留原有下界并兼容新旧原生委派、Owner 身份与工具接口；开发依赖仍精确固定，真实版本验收单独记录，允许后续 0.2.x 安装不代表其运行行为已经验证（[#2167](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2167)）。
