## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now shows how many messages are unread on each PersonaBot DM and Channel in Chats, in the same count chip the computer drawer uses: your accent colour for unread, amber for what needs you, grey for muted Channels, capped at 99+. A collapsed Channel section shows the total. Opening a conversation marks it read, on the phone and on your computer ([#1888](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1888)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的聊天列表里，每个 PersonaBot 私聊和频道现在都会显示未读消息数，样式和电脑抽屉里的计数 chip 一致：未读用主题色，需要你处理是琥珀色，已静音的频道是灰色，超过 99 显示 99+。折叠的频道分组会显示合计。打开会话就会标为已读，手机和电脑上同步生效（[#1888](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1888)）。
