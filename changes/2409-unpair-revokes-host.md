## en

- Unpairing a computer on the phone now also revokes the phone on that computer, so its old pairing stops working. If the computer can't be reached, the phone says so and points to Settings › Devices on the computer ([#2409](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2409)).

## zh

- 在手机上取消配对电脑时，会同时在那台电脑上撤销这台手机，旧的配对随即失效。连不上电脑时，手机会提示你去电脑的 设置 › 设备 里移除（[#2409](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2409)）。
