## en

- The mobile pairing and Activity filter sheets now open with the same glass title capsule as the first-run privacy notice, laid out like iOS 26 system sheets: close on the left, the title centred, and room for a confirm button on the right, the Settings sheet gets the same title capsule and still closes from its drag handle, and the Chinese Activity tab and its search, sort and empty states read 「活动」 throughout instead of mixing 「动态」 with English ([#2324](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2324)).

## zh

- 手机端的配对和活动筛选抽屉现在与首次启动的隐私说明一样，使用玻璃质感标题胶囊，布局参照 iOS 26 系统抽屉：左上角关闭、标题居中、右上角可放确认，设置抽屉也用同样的标题胶囊，仍然通过拖动条关闭；中文界面里的活动页及其搜索、排序和空状态统一叫「活动」，不再混用「动态」和英文（[#2324](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2324)）。
