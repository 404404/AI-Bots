## en

- On the phone's Chats and Activity headers, the Search, Settings and Filter buttons are now native buttons: Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later, and Material 3 icon buttons on Android. Earlier iOS keeps the current round buttons ([#2487](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2487)).

## zh

- 手机端聊天列表和 Activity 顶栏的搜索、设置、筛选按钮改用系统原生按钮：iOS 26 及以上为液态玻璃（Liquid Glass）按钮，Android 为 Material 3 图标按钮；更早的 iOS 保持原来的圆形按钮（[#2487](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2487)）。
