## en

- Panel title bars and panel frames in the Channel dock no longer re-render while a reply streams or another Bot's Activity changes. The dock now passes the Messages body and Channel header to the panels that show them, and keeps everything else stable ([#2450](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2450)).

## zh

- 频道停靠区的面板标题栏和面板容器，在回复流式输出或其他 Bot 的 Activity 变化时不再重新渲染。停靠区现在只把消息正文和频道标题交给显示它们的位置，其余内容保持不变（[#2450](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2450)）。
