## en

- Sessions now opens inside Messages, with a draggable split and visibility, side and proportions remembered per Channel. Existing layouts and shortcuts migrate; the Sessions shortcut restores a hidden Messages panel. Groups do not expose Bot-only Browser or Computer controls ([#2049](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2049)).
