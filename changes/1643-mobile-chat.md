## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a DM now groups consecutive messages from the same sender, shows a time label after a long gap, and puts the Bot's avatar, name and state in the header. While the Bot is thinking or working, a status strip sits above a new pill composer ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的私聊页现在会把同一发送者的连续消息合成一组，间隔较久时显示时间标签，顶栏显示 Bot 的头像、名字和状态。Bot 思考或工作时，新的胶囊输入框上方会出现状态条（[#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)）。
