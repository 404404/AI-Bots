## en

- Create a PersonaBot or a Channel from the DeepSeekBot mobile app. Tap the + button next to your avatar at the top right of Chats and pick New PersonaBot or New Channel and the full client opens on its create dialog ([#1854](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1854)).

## zh

- 在 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 里就能新建 PersonaBot 或频道。点聊天页右上角头像旁的 + 按钮，选新建 PersonaBot 或新建频道，完整客户端会直接打开对应的创建对话框（[#1854](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1854)）。
