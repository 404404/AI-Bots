## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, the conversation list now groups Channels by the Channel sections made on desktop, in the desktop's order with ungrouped Channels between sections. Sections collapse on the phone and still show their count and anything that needs you, Hidden Channels stay out, and a Host without sections keeps the flat list ([#1853](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1853)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的对话列表现在按桌面端建好的 Channel 分区分组，顺序和桌面一致，未分组的 Channel 留在分区之间。分区可以在手机上折叠，折叠后仍显示数量和需要你处理的内容；隐藏的 Channel 不会出现；没有分区的电脑仍是原来的平铺列表（[#1853](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1853)）。
