## en

- WeChat paired-owner conversations can be mirrored into the Bot’s local DM, with a separate option to forward local DM messages back to that account. Both switches default off and include clickable help. Mirroring reuses original content, follows content purge, and adds no second wake. Local approval, private-input and other control cards are not forwarded. Forwarding preserves the exact account boundary and stops unsent work when disabled ([#1983](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1983), [spec #1990](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1990)).

## zh

- 微信扫码绑定者的私聊可镜像到 Bot 的本地私聊，并可单独选择将本地私聊转发回该账户。两个开关均默认关闭，旁边提供可点击的说明。镜像复用原始内容、参与内容清除，不重复唤醒 Bot；本地审批、私密输入等交互卡片不会外发；转发保留具体账户边界，关闭后停止尚未发送的操作（[#1983](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1983)，[规格 #1990](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1990)）。
