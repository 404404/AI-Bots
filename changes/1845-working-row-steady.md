## en

- On iPhone, the Bot's "Thinking…" row under a streaming reply no longer flickers down into the message box for a frame and back. It stays at the end of the message list while the reply grows and scrolls away with the messages when you scroll up ([#1845](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1845)).
