## en

- Phone notifications from a Bot now look like chat messages: a group Channel shows the Channel picture and name with the Bot as the sender, and a DM shows the Bot's picture. On iOS this uses Communication Notifications; on Android the notification joins the Conversations section. Needs a new app build ([#2397](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2397)).

## zh

- 手机上的 Bot 通知现在显示为聊天消息：群聊显示群头像和群名，发送者是 Bot；私聊显示 Bot 的头像。iOS 使用通讯通知，Android 的通知会进入「对话」区。需要安装新的 App 版本（[#2397](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2397)）。
