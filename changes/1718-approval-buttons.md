## en

- In the DeepSeekBot mobile app, a Tool approval card shows Deny in red, and its Allow once, Always allow and Deny buttons stand out from the card in light and dark mode; buttons are borderless pills with a neutral black or white primary instead of the Bot's colour, and every button colour comes from shared design tokens ([#1718](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1718)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的工具审批卡片的「拒绝」用红色显示，「允许一次」「一直允许」「拒绝」按钮在浅色和深色模式下都和卡片背景区分开；按钮改成无描边的胶囊，主按钮用中性的黑或白，不再用 Bot 的颜色；所有按钮颜色都来自统一的 design token（[#1718](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1718)）。
