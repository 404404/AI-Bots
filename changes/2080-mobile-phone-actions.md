## en

- The mobile app now handles the remaining requests that used to need the desktop: allow an auto-rejected call once, accept or decline a Bot's group join request, and reply to an Assignment that is waiting on you or blocked so it resumes. These requests also send phone notifications (approvals and access requests, or questions and Assignments). A private input request now shows a card in chat that opens the secure form in the full client on the phone ([#2080](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2080)).

## zh

- 手机端现在可以处理之前必须回到电脑的请求：对被自动拒绝的调用“仍然允许这一次”、同意或拒绝 Bot 的入群申请、回复等待你或受阻的事项让它继续。这些请求也会推送到手机（归在“审批与授权请求”或“提问与事项”）。私密输入请求在聊天里显示卡片，可直接在手机上的完整客户端打开安全填写页（[#2080](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2080)）。
