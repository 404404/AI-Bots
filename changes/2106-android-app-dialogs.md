## en

- Android computer menus, unpair confirmations, creation choices and group confirmations now use rounded, themed app dialogs consistent with Settings. Important menu choices have tonal button containers and icons; destructive choices use a danger fill. Choice menus dismiss through the backdrop or Back without a redundant Cancel button. Confirmations retain a lower-emphasis Cancel; pending destructive actions cannot be submitted twice, stale menu callbacks cannot confirm a newer dialog, and failed actions stay available for retry ([#2106](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2106)).

## zh

- Android 的电脑菜单、取消配对、新建选项和群组确认框统一采用与设置页一致的圆角主题弹窗；关键菜单操作使用带图标的 Tonal 按钮，危险操作使用危险色底色。选择菜单去掉重复的取消按钮，点击空白处或返回即可关闭；确认框仍保留较弱的取消操作。执行中的危险操作不能重复提交，旧菜单回调不能确认新弹窗，失败后可重试（[#2106](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2106)）。
