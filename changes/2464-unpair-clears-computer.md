## en

- Unpairing a computer on the phone now also removes what it left behind: its delivered notifications, cached conversations, home-screen chat shortcuts and an environment filter set to it. A conversation that was open on it no longer switches to another computer's Bot ([#2464](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2464)).
