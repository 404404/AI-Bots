## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now corrects its icon badge and removes notifications for requests that were already resolved on the computer when you open it again, instead of keeping a stale badge and banner until something else changes. Pushed screens show a plain back chevron (read as "Back") instead of the internal "(tabs)" label ([#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)).

## zh

- 重新打开 DeepSeekBot 手机 App 时，App 图标角标会更新，已在电脑上处理的请求的通知也会被移除，不再一直留着过期的角标和通知。二级页面的返回按钮只显示箭头（读屏读作「返回」），不再显示内部的「(tabs)」字样（[#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)）。
