## en

- Personalized Home with the steward avatar, an editable global greeting name and four editable starter questions; Home hides model setup once its effective model is usable; Tob defaults to a localized name and the system Bot icon until customized, and long messages now remain expanded unless automatic folding is enabled in Messages settings ([#2081](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2081)).
- Preserve the complete split layout and live content when Home or other panels are hidden and reopened; panel icon/title targets now hide their panel on click while retaining drag behavior ([#2081](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2081)).

## zh

- Home 新增总管头像、可修改的全局用户名欢迎语和四条可编辑常用提问；有效模型可用后隐藏 Home 模型设置入口；总管未自定义时使用本地化名称及系统 Bot 图标，长消息默认完整展开，可在消息设置开启自动折叠（[#2081](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2081)）。
- 修复 Home 等面板关闭重开时的整组分割布局和内容保留；点击面板图标及标题可隐藏面板，同时保留拖拽能力（[#2081](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2081)）。
