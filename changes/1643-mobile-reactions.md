## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app shows reactions under DM messages, including a Bot's reaction-only acknowledgment, and updates them live. Long-press a message to react with 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 👀 ✅ or copy it, and tap a reaction to add or remove yours ([#1643](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1643)).
