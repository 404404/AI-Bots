## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app can customize each computer's header: Me → Customize header picks an avatar (its initial, a pixel portrait or a photo) and a banner (the theme colour, a solid or custom colour, a gradient, or a picture), with a live preview in light and dark mode ([#1866](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1866)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 可以自定义每台电脑的顶栏：在“我”→“自定义顶栏”里选头像（首字母、像素头像或照片）和顶栏背景（主题色、纯色或自定义颜色、渐变或图片），亮色和暗色模式下都有实时预览（[#1866](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1866)）。
