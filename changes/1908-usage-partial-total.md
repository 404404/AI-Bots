## en

- The Overview's token usage no longer shows "Unknown" for the whole week when some usage went unreported, for example rows kept from before an upgrade. The total now counts what was reported and is marked with **+**, and each day's bar shows its reported usage ([#1908](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1908)).

## zh
