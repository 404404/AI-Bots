## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app's Activity tab has filter chips for each section with its count, plus a Bot filter when more than one Bot is listed, and section headers stay pinned while you scroll. Posts are separated without divider lines, and handled items collapse to one line ([#1653](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1653)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 的「活动」页新增筛选条，每个分组带数量，列表里有多个 Bot 时还能按 Bot 筛选；滚动时分组标题会固定在顶部。条目之间不再用分隔线，已处理的条目收成一行（[#1653](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1653)）。
