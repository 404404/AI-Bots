## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app now gets a push notification when a PersonaBot asks for a Tool approval, a formal Human question or private input, or mentions or replies to you, even with the app closed. A notification names the PersonaBot and what it needs and never carries the message, command or question; tapping it opens that request on the right computer, and the app icon badge counts unresolved requests. Notifications from a request resolved elsewhere stop, revoking a phone deletes its notification address, and the app's Notifications screen turns each kind on or off and sends a test. The app now keeps every paired computer and switches between them ([ADR-0202](../docs/adr/0202-phone-push-is-a-content-free-projection-of-the-human-inbox.md), [#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 现在会在 PersonaBot 请求工具审批、正式提问或私密输入，或提及、回复你时推送通知，App 关闭时也能收到。通知只写是哪个 PersonaBot、需要什么，不包含消息、命令或问题内容；点按会在对应电脑上打开该请求，App 图标角标显示未处理请求数。已在其他地方处理的请求不再推送；撤销手机会删除它的通知地址；App 的「通知」页可按类型开关通知并发送测试通知。App 现在会保存每台配对的电脑并可在它们之间切换（[ADR-0202](../docs/adr/0202-phone-push-is-a-content-free-projection-of-the-human-inbox.md)，[#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)）。
