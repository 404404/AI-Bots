## en

- In the mobile app, switching computers now happens in a left drawer: tap the computer name at the top of Home, or swipe right from the left edge of Home or Activity. Each computer shows whether it is online, with a red dot on its avatar when it has unread conversations and one badge that counts items needing you first, or unread conversations when nothing needs you, and a ⋯ menu to unpair it; the footer keeps "Pair a new computer" and Notifications ([#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)).

## zh

- 手机应用改为在左侧抽屉里切换电脑：点首页顶部的电脑名称，或在首页、动态页从左边缘向右滑即可打开。每台电脑会显示是否在线，有未读会话时头像右上角显示红点，角标优先显示等你处理的数量，没有时显示未读会话数，⋯ 菜单可取消配对；底部固定“配对新电脑”和“通知”（[#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)）。
