## en

- Added "Open full client" to the DeepSeekBot mobile app: the Host's full Web Client opens in an in-app modal, already signed in with the paired phone, for everything the app does not show natively yet. It can land on Bot mode, a PersonaBot's DM or a private input request, and revoking the phone in Settings → Devices ends the in-app Web Client at once too ([ADR-0199](../docs/adr/0199-paired-devices-reach-the-host-through-a-same-listener-device-gateway.md), [#1654](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1654)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 新增「打开完整网页端」：在 App 内以弹窗打开 Host 的完整网页端，用已配对的手机身份直接登录，用来访问 App 还没原生支持的功能。它可以直接落到 Bot 模式、某个 PersonaBot 的私聊或一条私密输入请求；在「设置 → 设备」撤销这台手机后，App 内的网页端也会立即失效（[ADR-0199](../docs/adr/0199-paired-devices-reach-the-host-through-a-same-listener-device-gateway.md)，[#1654](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1654)）。
