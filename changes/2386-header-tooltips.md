## en

- Buttons in desktop panel headers, such as the Messages panel's sub-panel toggles, Channel files, Bot Inbox, Browser and Computer access, the Channels list and Activity Center, now show their tooltip as soon as the pointer or keyboard focus reaches them, and a panel title explains that it can be dragged to move the panel or clicked to hide it ([#2386](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2386)).

## zh

- 桌面端面板标题栏的按钮（如消息面板的各子面板开关、频道文件、Bot 收件箱、浏览器和电脑权限、频道列表和动态中心）在鼠标悬停或键盘聚焦时立刻显示提示；面板标题也会提示可按住拖动移动面板、点击隐藏面板（[#2386](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2386)）。
