## en

- The DeepSeekBot mobile app's Me tab is gone. Tap your avatar at the top right of the list to open Settings, which holds the current computer and its status, your computers, notifications, the full client and unpairing, plus Customize header under Interface. Home is now called Chats, so the tab bar shows Chats and Activity ([#1850](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1850)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 手机 App 去掉了“我”这个 Tab。点列表右上角你的头像打开设置，里面有当前电脑及其状态、电脑列表、通知、完整客户端和取消配对，界面分组里有自定义顶栏。首页改名为聊天，底部只剩聊天和动态两个 Tab（[#1850](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1850)）。
