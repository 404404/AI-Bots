## en

- Added manual compaction: `/compact` in a Bot DM's composer, IM `/compact` (`/压缩`) and `deepseekbot bot-compact <bot-id>` compact the Bot's Orchestrator Session now through DSH, report tokens before and after, and refuse while the Bot is working ([#2332](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2332)).

## zh

- 新增手动压缩：Bot 私信输入框的 `/compact`、IM `/compact`（`/压缩`）和 `deepseekbot bot-compact <bot-id>` 会立即通过 DSH 压缩 Bot 的编排会话，显示压缩前后的 token 数；Bot 正在工作时拒绝执行（[#2332](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2332)）。
