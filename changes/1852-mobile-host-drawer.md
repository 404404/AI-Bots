## en

- In the mobile app, switching computers now happens in a left drawer: tap the computer name at the top of Home, or swipe right from the left edge of Home or Activity. Each computer shows whether it is online and how many items need you, and the drawer ends with "Pair a new computer" ([#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)).

## zh

- 手机应用改为在左侧抽屉里切换电脑：点首页顶部的电脑名称，或在首页、动态页从左边缘向右滑即可打开。每台电脑会显示是否在线以及等你处理的数量，列表末尾是“配对新电脑”（[#1852](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1852)）。
